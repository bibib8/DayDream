Daydream Digital Diary

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Daydream Digital Diary mobile app ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use it. The App is a personal digital diary and scrapbook that lets you write journal entries, decorate pages, add photos and track your daily mood.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1

Information we collect

Information you give us

Data Examples Why we need it
Account information Email address, password, pen name To create your account, sign you in and show your name in the App. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is stored only in hashed (encrypted) form; we never see it.
Profile picture An emoji, or a photo you choose from your gallery or take with your camera To display your avatar in the App.
Diary content Diary names, cover designs, entry titles and text, weather and soundtrack notes, stickers and page decorations To save your diary and sync it to your account so you can see it again after signing in.
Photos Images you add to diary entries To show them on your diary pages.
Mood logs The mood you choose for a day (e.g. happy, calm, tired) and an optional note To build your monthly mood calendar.

Diary entries and mood logs can contain sensitive personal information about your feelings and daily life. You decide what to write, and this content is used only to provide the App's features to you.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: When the App communicates with our servers, our hosting provider may record technical information such as your IP address, device/browser type, and the date and time of requests. This is used for security, preventing abuse and keeping the service running.
  • Font loading: The App may download fonts from Google Fonts servers. When this happens, Google receives your IP address as part of a normal web request.

We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not collect your precise location, contacts or call logs.

Device permissions

  • Photos / gallery: Used only when you choose to add a photo to an entry or set a profile picture.
  • Camera: Used only when you choose to take a photo for your profile picture.

The App only accesses the specific photo you select. You can refuse or revoke these permissions in your device settings at any time; the rest of the App will keep working.

2

How we use your information

  • To provide the App's core features: accounts, writing and saving diary entries, photos and the mood calendar.
  • To keep your data synced to your account across sign-ins and devices.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To respond to your questions or requests.

We do not sell your personal information, use your diary content for advertising, or share it with advertisers.

3

How your information is stored and shared

We use Supabase (supabase.com) as our cloud backend for authentication, database and file storage. Supabase processes your data on our behalf only to run the App. Its servers may be located outside your country. Data is sent between the App and Supabase over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Each user's account, diaries, entries and mood logs are protected by access rules so that only the signed-in owner can read or change them.

About photos:
Photos you upload (diary photos and profile pictures) are stored in cloud storage and given a long, randomly generated web address. These addresses are not listed or searchable, but anyone who obtains the exact link can open the image. Please avoid uploading photos you would not want anyone else to see.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or in connection with a transfer of the App to a new owner (in which case this policy will continue to apply).

4

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete a diary or an entry, it is removed from our database. When you delete your account, we delete your account and associated data as described below. Copies in routine backups held by our hosting provider may persist for a limited period before they are overwritten.

5

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your data in two ways:

  • In the App: Go to Settings → Delete Account. This removes your diaries, entries, mood logs and profile.
  • By email: Send a request from your account's email address to support.daydream@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Daydream account". We will delete your account, sign-in credentials, uploaded photos and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also delete individual entries or diaries at any time inside the App.

6

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA) or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support.daydream@gmail.com. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and per-user access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong password that you do not use elsewhere.

9

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify you in the App.
